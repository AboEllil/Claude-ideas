How the verdict is made

Indicators (daily bars)

  • Trend: ML Adaptive SuperTrend (AlgoAlpha), ATR 10, factor 3, 100-bar k-means.
  • Momentum: Squeeze Momentum (LazyBear) 20 / 1.5. Bright green means positive and rising.
  • Confirmation: Lorentzian Classification (jdehorty) green arrow in the last 5 days.
  • Score out of 100: trend 40, squeeze 20, Lorentzian 20, market structure 20.
  • A buy signal on a thinly traded asset (under $5M a day, or EGP 1M on EGX) is shown as Hold and flagged.

What the tests showed

On the Egyptian market this system beat buy and hold. On large US stocks it trailed buy and hold in return but roughly halved the worst drop. Exiting on the red arrow worked better than tighter stops. Past results do not guarantee future ones, and this is not financial advice.

Halal screen

US stocks worth over $2 billion pass an AAOIFI-style screen: no interest-based finance, alcohol, tobacco, gambling, pork, weapons, hotels or entertainment; debt and cash each under 30% of market value. Egyptian stocks are screened by business type only (no conventional banks, brokers, tobacco or hotels): check debt with the EGX33 Shariah index. Crypto leaves out stablecoins, wrapped tokens, meme coins, interest-bearing tokens and margin-exchange tokens; scholars differ on whether crypto is permissible. Check each asset with a Shariah screener before buying.
