How the verdict is made

Rule for each market

  • US stocks and ETFs: hold while the price is above its 200-day average. Sell when it closes below. Tested 2021–today: 28% a year, Sharpe 1.37, worst drop −24% (your arrow system: 14%, 0.76, −27%).
  • Egypt: swing trend stack. Buy when the 50-day EMA is above the 200-day, ADX is above 20 and the price closes above its 20-day high. Sell on a 3 × ATR trailing stop or a close below the 50-day EMA. 2021–today: 40% a year, Sharpe 1.61, worst drop −30%; also best in 2016–2020.
  • Crypto: your AlgoAlpha green arrow + bright green squeeze, only when the weekly AlgoAlpha trend is green. Sell on the red arrow. It halved the worst drop (−30% vs −60%) at a lower return.

Verdicts

  • Strong buy: new entry in the last 3 days, confirmed by the weekly trend and by your arrow, a volume surge (Egypt) or a Lorentzian arrow (crypto).
  • Buy: new entry, or your arrow + bright squeeze with the weekly trend green inside an uptrend.
  • Hold: in an uptrend. Keep it with the stop shown. At the take-profit price, sell half and move the stop to your entry.
  • Sell: the rule exited in the last 3 days. Avoid: no uptrend.
  • A buy on a thinly traded asset (under $5M a day, or EGP 1M) is shown as Hold.

Crypto ML mood and adaptive trend

ML mood: a model trained on 83,000 coin-days reads the crypto market's mood (Bitcoin's last 30 days, stablecoin growth, Fed liquidity, Bitcoin network activity) and each coin's own data. It cannot tell which coin will rise, but taking the crypto rule's signals only when the mood is positive gave a steadier result in both test periods. Adaptive trend: each coin's AlgoAlpha setting (factor / ATR) re-chosen every January from its last 3 years, and whether that trend is green. It passed on the first 33 coins but not on the re-test with 75 coins (no gain in 2019–2020), so it is information only. The ML mood passed both times. Bitcoin timing: hold Bitcoin only while US buyers on Coinbase pay a premium (passed). All three are tracked forward from November 2026.

Strength of a buy

Each buy counts 5 confirmations: weekly trend green, daily AlgoAlpha trend green, squeeze momentum bright green, a Lorentzian green arrow in the last 5 days, and a supportive backdrop (the market above its 200-day average for US and Egypt, Fed liquidity rising for crypto). Strong = 4–5, Medium = 3, Weak = 0–2. On Egyptian stocks and crypto, stronger signals won more often in both 2016–2020 and 2021–today. On US stocks they did not, so there the strength is shown for information and every buy is treated the same. "Past signals like this" is how often signals of the same market, rule and strength won since 2016, with the same exits (half at +2 ATR, then stop at entry) and costs.

ML rank

0–100 within each market: how likely the model thinks the asset is to beat its market over the next 20 trading days. It was retrained each year on past data only and tested on 2019–today: a small but steady edge (AUC 0.53–0.55). Use it to choose between signals on the same day, not as a signal on its own.

Halal screen

Every US and Egyptian stock shown passed a full AAOIFI-style check from its latest financial statements: no interest-based finance, alcohol, tobacco, gambling, pork, weapons, hotels or entertainment; total debt under 30% and cash plus short-term investments under 30% of market value; interest income under 5% of revenue. Stocks that fail, or whose figures are missing, are left out. Islamic banks are accepted on their own Sharia board's certification. "Confirmed" means the stock is also held by at least one certified Shariah fund (SPUS, HLAL, SPTE, MNZL, Amana, Iman or Azzad), from their latest holdings filed with the SEC; "Screened" means it passed our check only. Our check is stricter than the funds' (it uses today's market value and all debt), so a few stocks the funds hold are left out. Zoya and Musaffa could not be checked from here: confirm there before buying. Crypto leaves out stablecoins, wrapped and staked tokens, meme coins, interest-bearing tokens and margin-exchange tokens; scholars differ on whether crypto is permissible. Check each asset with a Shariah screener before buying. Past results do not guarantee future ones, and this is not financial advice.
