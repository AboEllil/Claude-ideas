## Verdict across timeframes
- **Lorentzian Classification**: loses money on 1h after costs (-4%/yr, ~30 trades per stock per year).
  Its good 4h result on the top 20 (Sharpe 1.63) disappears on ranks 21-100 (0.22) - luck, not edge.
- **Squeeze Momentum**: flat to negative on every timeframe and both stock groups.
- **ML Adaptive SuperTrend**: the most consistent. Similar Sharpe to buy & hold with roughly half the drawdown
  on 1h (top 20), daily and weekly; weaker on 1h for ranks 21-100.
- **Smart Money Concepts**: best on **4h** - Sharpe 1.46 / 1.38 (top 20 / ranks 21-100) vs buy & hold 1.68 / 1.44,
  with drawdowns of only -14% / -5%, but in the market just ~37% of the time, so returns are much lower.
- **Confluence (ours)**: middle of the pack; its rotation version is the best "ours" variant on 4h and weekly.
- **Nothing beat buy & hold on return** on any timeframe. 1h/4h cover only ~2.4 years of one bull regime.
